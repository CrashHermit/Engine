How to interpret this comparison

  • Both warp methods are full 3D fields sampled on the unit sphere; the cube slice shows original query coordinates at z = 0.
  • No-fold volume regions can still produce disconnected patches on the sphere.
  • Sequential additive shears are invertible and volume-preserving in ideal arithmetic.
  • Whole-warp finite-difference determinants become unreliable under strong stretching: they can report negative values even for invertible shears. They are resolution-dependent estimates, not a folding test. Additive shears have determinant 1 algebraically; the reverse-operation round trip checks numerical reversibility.
  • Diagnostics describe the plate warp. Patch counts use sampled mesh adjacency; tiny unresolved features can change with resolution.
  • This is an experiment only; production behavior is unchanged.
