Crust material and continuous elevation

The crust partition gives discrete regions; continuous regional memberships shape the coastal profile. Tectonic response and independent 3D terrain variation add to the sampled elevation. All elevation percentages are relative to the selected planet radius; the globe is only a sampling surface.

How to read these fields

Crust labels
Discrete continental/oceanic identity, shown at hex samples.

Continental influence
A continuous scalar from 0 to 1. Oceanic influence is 1 − continental.

Subduction preference
A separate seed-derived vector field. The image shows its magnitude, not a boundary category.

Coastline
The coastal contribution's nominal sea level is anchored at the configured continental influence. Tectonic relief and final world-space noise can move the total elevation's zero crossings.
