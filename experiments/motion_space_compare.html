Regional source fields and scalar warp composition

This diagnostic compares a volumetric, algebraic region-overlap field in source coordinates with that same scalar field composed through the shear warp. Euler rotations, seed-bisector normals, pair motion, and relief are evaluated in Cartesian source space. The warped result is sampled as G_source(W(p)); vectors and normals are not transported.

Shared settings

Regional membership uses normalized products of smooth pairwise bisector transitions. Sparse pair influence is 4 w_i w_j, including every positive candidate pair without a physical-adjacency filter. Pair signed coordinates and normals refer to ordered seed bisectors. Elevation is dimensionless diagnostic relief, not a complete geology pipeline or physical height.
