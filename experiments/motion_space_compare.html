Motion spaces: warp motion or warp relief?

A matched diagnostic compares three ways to combine a continuous warped Voronoi partition, Euler-pole rotations, boundary orientation and additive relief. The same seeds, rotations, source-space face supports and relief profiles are used in every case.

How to read the three cases

  • A · World-space rigid motion: each region uses its Euler-pole velocity at the queried world coordinate. The source face normal is pushed into the world frame by the warp Jacobian.
  • B · Warped source motion: rigid source-space velocities are transported through the inverse warp Jacobian; the boundary normal is transformed independently. This creates a continuous procedural world-space motion approximation, not rigid plates away from the boundary.
  • C · Warped source relief: motion and each pair's profile are evaluated in source coordinates, then the scalar relief is sampled at the warped coordinate. Its vector panel is deliberately an unwarped source-space reference, not the same z = 0 sheet as A/B.
  • Opening is signed per pair: negative means convergence; positive means separation. Sliding is the magnitude parallel to that pair's normal. B and C preserve each pair's opening sign, but their magnitudes and sliding differ; summing overlapping pairs can still change the aggregate sign.
  • The normal is the oriented extension of a clipped source Voronoi face into its compact influence band. Near clipped face edges it is not necessarily the direction to the closest point on that face. Supports may overlap; every active pair contributes independently.
  • The shared support width is measured in source Cartesian coordinates. It does not imply fixed physical width on the displayed observation sphere. All interactions are computed in Cartesian 3D; the sphere is only a visualization surface, and transported vectors are not generally tangent to it.
  • The relief recipe is intentionally uniform-continental and dimensionless: compression uses the existing broad/crest collision profile (65%/35%, crest fraction 0.2), opening uses the existing continental divergence profile, and sliding adds 15% of the existing shear profile. It is not the full geology/elevation pipeline, and profiles are not retuned per case.
  • Warp Jacobians are approximated by central differences of individual scalar shears. The noise sampler uses float32 coordinates, so step-size sensitivity is reported; sensitive results are exploratory and do not justify adopting a motion model by appearance alone.
  • This temporary comparison does not change production generation, production derivatives, or production defaults.
