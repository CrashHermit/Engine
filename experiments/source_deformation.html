Source-space local deformation

Static, volumetric Cartesian regional overlap defines three independent scalar fields—convergence, divergence, and shear—plus a dimensionless diagnostic relief. Source-space response is evaluated per overlapping seed pair, weighted once by algebraic overlap, and the completed scalar is sampled through the existing warp. It is not a final rigid world velocity.

Coordinate sheets and model scope

Source XY uses z = 0. World XY also uses z = 0, but these are different coordinate sheets: W(p) generally has nonzero source z. Source globes sample the source field on unit-sphere observation points; warped globes sample the same 3D field at W(p) on the world observation sphere. The sphere is not projected back onto itself, and geometry of displayed spheres remains undisplaced except for radial relief display exaggeration.

The regional transition half-width and response-profile width are independent source-coordinate parameters; both are stretched under the warp. Euler-pole rotations retain the original seed-anchored parameters; they are static source-motion calculation inputs, not transported or asserted to be final-world velocities. No spherical projection, time simulation, velocity transport, Jacobian, or numerical/analytical derivative is used. The native OpenSimplex sampler and normal worldgen CLI are unchanged.

Elevation is dimensionless diagnostic relief, not metres or a complete geological planet. The uniform-continental recipe applies existing collision/divergence/shear profiles per overlapping seed pair; it does not model crust, coasts, sea level, or material-specific geology. Color scales are shared across seeds, source/warped cases and sampled surfaces; these new C-only scales differ from the historical A/B/C gallery.

Component metrics

Slice, sphere, and volume-probe minimum, maximum, and RMS are measured separately.

Volumetric interaction distributions

Uniform 32,768-point cube probe, shared by all seeds and cases. Compact regional transitions produce zero overlap away from interaction zones. Seed-bisector normal alignment is reported for all candidate seed pairs, not claimed Voronoi faces or confirmed physical adjacency. Shear dominance is a measurement, not an implementation bug or a claim of unbiased geology. Source/warped differences reflect changed sampled source locations, not changed Euler velocities.
